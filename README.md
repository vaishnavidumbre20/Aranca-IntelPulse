
Aranca IntelPulse

 AI-Powered Competitive Intelligence Agent & Dashboard

Aranca IntelPulse is an AI-powered competitive intelligence platform designed to automate competitor monitoring, analyze business developments, and generate actionable insights for strategic decision-making.

## Key Features

- Automated competitor intelligence monitoring
- AI-generated executive intelligence briefs
- Business updates, deals, partnerships, and M&A tracking
- Competitor and geographic intelligence filtering
- Source-linked intelligence with publication dates
- AI-powered multi-document report analysis
- Executive dashboard for actionable business insights

 Technology Stack

- Python
- Streamlit
- Google Gemini API
- Pandas

Supported Geographies

United States | Saudi Arabia | UAE | India | Japan

 
Designed to support competitive intelligence research and strategic decision-making through AI-powered analysis and structured intelligence reporting.
